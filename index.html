The roster

Three players, signed through 2030.

NoeyTRC

Captain

Mainly a passive playmaker. Eccentric, fast and an exquisite dribbler, Noey builds his game around ground dribbles and flicks, which makes him the perfect relayer for aerial players.

Contract until 2030Full profile →

BAZOOK

Player

A mildly offensive player and the perfect double to Noey’s passiveness. The two have spent 2 years mastering the art of tiki-taka: orbiting on points, building their own vocabulary and techniques.

Contract until 2030Full profile →

Nirox

New recruit

A balanced player built around complicated aerial skills, pinches and flicks. He has a higher rank than the other two and masters aerial finesse touches. He has yet to prove himself consistently, but the captain calls him the “perfect player to finish our roster”.

Contract until 2030Full profile →
